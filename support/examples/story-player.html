Chalk — Maya's call survives a crash

A user story you can press play on. Maya is mid video-call when her call server crashes. In the old world she was thrown to an error page and had to rejoin by hand. After the change, Chalk restores her call from its session lease before she can finish a sigh. Same hiccup, two worlds — watch both.
